# YouTube Shorts scripts — K-Pop Random Dance Generator

Two vertical (9:16, 1080x1920) Shorts, ≤30s each, for kpopgenerator.cloud.
Both are screen-recording first: no face needed, no voice needed if you prefer
on-screen text + trending audio (recommended for reach in the RDP community).

Shared production notes
- Record the site on mobile viewport (or crop desktop to 9:16) so it reads as a phone app.
- Keep every caption inside the middle 60% of the frame — title/CTA overlays eat the edges.
- Burn in captions. ~70% of Shorts viewers watch muted on first pass.
- Use a currently-trending K-pop sound at ~15-20% under your VO, or as the only audio.
- Cut every 1.5-2.5s. No shot longer than 3s.
- End frame holds the URL for the full last 2s — that is where the clicks come from.

### Why these keywords
The community searches in three shapes: the event name ("random play dance", "rdp"), the intent
("how to make a random dance mix"), and the group name. Video 1 targets shape one with the hook,
Video 2 targets shape two with a title that is a literal search query, and both descriptions carry
the group names so they surface on group-specific searches.

### Posting notes
- Post Video 2 first — the how-to earns saves and comments, which is what Shorts ranks on.
- Post Video 1 three to four days later; it retargets the same audience with a different hook.
- Cross-post both to TikTok, Instagram Reels, and r/kpophelp / random dance Discords. The RDP
  community mostly discovers tools through Discord and TikTok, and that traffic feeds the YouTube
  ranking back.
