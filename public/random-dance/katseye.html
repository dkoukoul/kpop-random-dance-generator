How to use these KATSEYE mixes

Press Open in builder on any mix. The songs and their cut points load into the builder on the home page in the order shown here. Drop songs you don't know, move songs up or down, or nudge a start or end time in the editor. Press Shuffle now for a random order (that is the point of a random play dance), then Generate mix to get one MP3 with a 5-second countdown before every song.

Want to keep a set? Save as project file saves it as a .json file. Open it later in the builder from Project → Open a saved file, or send it to whoever is running your RPD event.

Where these cut points come from

Each clip is the segment most commonly chosen for that video by people using this generator. In practice that lands on the chorus or the dance break, because that is the part of a KATSEYE song a random play dance crowd knows and can actually follow. The pool is rebuilt as usage changes, so the mixes shift over time rather than sitting frozen at whatever was popular when the page was written.

Lyric videos, audio rips and reaction uploads are filtered out, and each song appears only once even when several uploads of it exist, so a mix never plays you the same choreography twice.
