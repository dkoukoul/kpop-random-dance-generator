Where these cut points come from

Each clip below is the segment most commonly chosen for that video by people using this generator. In practice that lands on the chorus or the dance break, because that is the part of a TWICE song a random play dance crowd knows and can actually follow. The pool is rebuilt as usage changes, so the mixes shift over time rather than sitting frozen at whatever was popular when the page was written.

Lyric videos, audio rips and reaction uploads are filtered out, and each song appears only once even when several uploads of it exist, so a mix never plays you the same choreography twice.
