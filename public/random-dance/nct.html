How to use these NCT mixes

Press Open in generator on any mix. The songs and their timings load straight into the generator on the home page, where you can drop tracks you don't know, drag the order around, or nudge a start time before generating the audio file. The songs open in a random order, and Shuffle gives you a new one whenever you like — which is the point of a random play dance.

Prefer to keep a set? Download .json saves it as a project file you can re-import later from the generator's Import button, or send to whoever is running your RPD event.

Where these cut points come from

Each clip below is the segment most commonly chosen for that video by people using this generator. In practice that lands on the chorus or the dance break, because that is the part of a NCT song a random play dance crowd knows and can actually follow. The pool is rebuilt as usage changes, so the mixes shift over time rather than sitting frozen at whatever was popular when the page was written.

Lyric videos, audio rips and reaction uploads are filtered out, and each song appears only once even when several uploads of it exist, so a mix never plays you the same choreography twice.
