What is a random play dance?

A random play dance (RPD) plays short segments of K-pop songs back to back in an order nobody knows in advance. When a song you know comes on, you step into the middle and dance it. It is the standard format for K-pop dance meet-ups in parks and convention halls, and it works just as well alone at home as a practice drill.

The mixes here are starting points. Open any of them in the random dance generator, cut songs you don't want, add your own songs from YouTube, and generate a single MP3 with a 3- or 5-second countdown before every song.
