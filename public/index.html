    About K-Pop Random Dance Generator

    Our free online K-Pop Random Dance Generator is the ultimate tool for K-Pop fans who love to practice their favorite choreographies. Whether you're preparing for a K-Pop dance cover, a public Random Play Dance (RPD) event, or just having fun at home, our tool helps you create the perfect practice mix in seconds.

    How It Works

    Simply add YouTube URLs from your favorite K-Pop groups like BTS, BLACKPINK, NewJeans, TWICE, Stray Kids, SEVENTEEN, aespa, (G)I-DLE, IVE, LE SSERAFIM, ITZY, Red Velvet, NCT, TXT, Enhypen, ATEEZ, Mamamoo, EXO, GOT7 and more. Set custom start and end times for each song segment, and our generator will automatically combine them with professional 5-second countdown transitions.

    Key Features

    • YouTube Integration: Extract any segment from K-Pop music videos and dance practices
    • Automatic Countdowns: Professional 5-second transitions between songs
    • Shuffle Mode: Randomize song order for true random dance practice
    • Drag & Drop: Easily reorder your playlist
    • Band Variety Tracking: Ensure diverse practice with multiple K-Pop groups
    • Export/Import Projects: Save and share your dance mixes
    • 100% Free: No registration required, unlimited generations
