      About K-Pop Random Dance Generator

      Make a random play dance (RPD) mix in minutes: add songs from YouTube, choose the part of each song, and get one MP3 with a 5-second countdown before every song. Free, no sign-up.
      How it works
      1. Search YouTube or paste a link to add songs.
      2. Pick the part of each song you want to dance to.
      3. Shuffle, then generate one MP3 with a 5-second countdown before every song.
      Key features
      • Search YouTube or paste a link: any song, from BTS and BLACKPINK to the newest groups
      • Exact start and end for every song, or the popular cut other hosts use
      • A 5-second countdown before every song, all in one MP3
      • Shuffle now: the order on screen is the order of your mix; reorder with ↑ ↓
      • Download a setlist with the start time of every song
      • Saves in your browser as you work; save to a file to keep or share it
      Perfect for

      Random play dance events, K-pop dance cover practice, studio warm-ups and solo training at home. You choose the exact part of every song, so the set stays on the parts people know and skips long intros.

      Common questions

      What is a K-Pop Random Dance Generator?
      An online tool that makes random dance practice mixes from short parts of many K-pop songs, with a countdown before every song, so you can practise choreography from BTS, BLACKPINK, NewJeans, TWICE and more.

      How do I create a random dance practice mix?
      Add songs from YouTube, set the start and end of each part, and press Generate. You get one MP3 with a 5-second countdown before every song.

      Which K-Pop groups can I use?
      Any group on YouTube, including BTS, BLACKPINK, NewJeans, TWICE, Stray Kids, SEVENTEEN, aespa, (G)I-DLE, IVE, LE SSERAFIM, ITZY, Red Velvet, NCT, TXT, ENHYPEN and ATEEZ.

      Is it free?
      Yes. Make as many mixes as you like, with no sign-up.

      Enjoying the tool?

      It's free and always will be. Support on Ko-fi helps pay for the servers.
